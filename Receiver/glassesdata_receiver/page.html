    Look at the board through the glasses

    The board fills the screen at its real size. A panel beside it says what to do next.

    1. Put the glasses on and start the glasses preview in GlassesData.
    2. Sit 30 to 60 cm from the screen and press Show the board.
    3. Move your head so the board appears in every part of your view, near and far, straight on and at an angle. Hold still for a moment at each spot.
    4. At 25 views press Solve. Esc brings you back here.
